Started with one oven and a Sunday habit

Hearth & Honey began as a wood-fired oven in a backyard and a standing Sunday invitation to neighbors. Three years and one small storefront later, the oven's still the center of the room — everything else was built around it.

"We don't do fast food. We do slow mornings done properly, for people willing to sit for a minute." — Dara Kessler, owner & baker
